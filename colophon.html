How Our Duo Project Was Built

This website was built collaboratively by our two-person team using Visual Studio Code, adhering strictly to semantic HTML standards without frameworks or generators. We divided the pages to showcase the full retail experience of the Astana Mega Silkway store, combining brand background, practical garment guides, seasonal visual showcases, and interactive feedback tools.

Maintaining a cohesive layout and rigorous semantic structure across independently developed pages required careful coordination. We utilized clear semantic landmarks like <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, and <aside> to ensure the document outline remains fully accessible and transparent.

Every page was tested using the W3C Markup Validation Service. Reaching the status of «No errors or warnings to show» confirmed that our syntax, meta declarations, and nesting rules are fully compliant.
