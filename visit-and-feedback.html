This page exists to let a visitor either book a visit to the Stradivarius store or leave feedback about a visit they already made. The form below covers both purposes at once: a visitor picks their goal — fitting, consultation, or feedback — using a radio button group, then fills in their contact details and, if relevant, an optional comment. The form fields were chosen to cover the required input types for this assignment while still making sense for a real store visit. The text, email, and phone fields collect contact information; the date field lets a visitor pick a preferred visit date; the number field asks how many people are coming; the select lets them indicate which department they are interested in; and the checkbox confirms consent to data processing. The textarea is there for any additional comment a visitor wants to add, such as details about their feedback or a specific request for the fitting. Since there is no server behind this form yet, submitting it does nothing at this stage — that is explained directly in a comment inside the form itself. Server-side handling will be added later in the course.

How it works:

  1. Fill in the form below
  2. Choose a convenient visit date
  3. Wait for a reply (in reality — by email/phone)
